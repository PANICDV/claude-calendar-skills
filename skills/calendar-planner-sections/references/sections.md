# How to build the sections

## Criteria
- **4–8 sections in the plan** (plus 1–2 for reminders). Each section is a calendar the
  person creates, imports and colors by hand, so every extra section costs them work.
- **Group by kind of attention or context**, not by single task:
  - what needs deep focus and is a priority (thesis, main project);
  - technical work or learning (servers, tools, courses);
  - what depends on business hours (banks, offices, calls, payments);
  - personal organization (email, files, planning, planner design);
  - income and investing (markets, sales);
  - routine and home (sleep, meals, exercise, kids, pets, cleaning);
  - flexible slots.
- **A big priority can have its own section** even if it is a single topic (for example
  "🎓 Thesis"), so it is visible how much of the week it takes.
- Use names that start with an emoji: the calendar's emoji matches its events' emoji.
- If the person works outside the home, fixed work and commutes can go in "💼 Work".

## Colors
Google Calendar palette (English names and hex). Besides the preset colors, Google lets you
click "+" and paste the exact hex.

| Name | Hex | Suggested use |
|---|---|---|
| Tomato | #D50000 | Priority number 1 |
| Flamingo | #E67C73 | Reminders |
| Tangerine | #F4511E | Errands, banks, dated payments |
| Banana | #F6BF26 | Money, investing, sales |
| Sage | #33B679 | Routine and home |
| Basil | #0B8043 | Health, sport (if separate from routine) |
| Peacock | #039BE5 | Personal organization |
| Blueberry | #3F51B5 | Work or job |
| Lavender | #7986CB | Secondary studies or courses |
| Grape | #8E24AA | Technical projects and learning |
| Graphite | #616161 | Flexible slots |

Rules:
- Priorities get strong colors (Tomato, Grape, Blueberry) and are never repeated.
- Routine in a soft color (Sage), because it takes a lot of space and should not dominate visually.
- Flexible slots always in gray (Graphite): they read as "usable free space".
- Do not give similar colors to sections that tend to sit together (Tomato and Tangerine on the same day, for example); if that happens, change one.
- If the person already chose colors before, respect them.

## Example sections (remote professional with a side project)

| # | Section | Color | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🚀 Main project | Tomato | Deliverables, milestones, deadlines |
| 2 | 💼 Work | Blueberry | Working hours, meetings, commutes |
| 3 | 🧩 Learning | Grape | Courses, tools, technical practice |
| 4 | 📈 Finances | Banana | Budget, investing, sales |
| 5 | 🏦 Banks and errands | Tangerine | Banks, insurance, taxes, appointments |
| 6 | 🏡 Routine and home | Sage | Sleep, exercise, meals, pets, school, cleaning |
| 7 | 🔄 Flexible slots | Graphite | Gaps for rescheduling |
| R | 🔔 Reminders | Flamingo | Monthly bills, cleaning every N days |
