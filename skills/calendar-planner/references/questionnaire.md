# Questionnaire in blocks

Ask one block per message, as a numbered list, so the person can answer by number
("1. weekly, 2. from the 3rd to the 11th…"). Before each block, remove what you already know
from the conversation or memory and confirm it in one line. Tell them they can answer
"n/a" or "not sure" and that you will estimate whatever is missing.

Adapt tone and language to the person. The questions below are a guide, not a script.

---

## Block 1: Type of plan and period
1. What do you need: a **daily, weekly, short-period (2–3 weeks), long-period (1–2 months) or quarterly** plan? Or **just recurring reminders**?
2. From which date and until when?
3. City or time zone?
4. Which calendar do you use: Google, Apple or Outlook?
5. How often do you want to review and adjust the plan (for example, every Friday)?

## Block 2: Fixed routine
1. **Sleep**: how many hours do you sleep, and when do you usually go to bed and get up? Is sleeping less on some days OK?
2. **Morning**: do you exercise, have breakfast, or follow a routine before starting the day? How long does it take?
3. **Fixed work or study**: days, hours, on-site or remote?
4. **Commutes**: time each way and means of transport.
5. **Meals**: lunch and dinner times. Do they sometimes run long?
6. **Children or dependents**: school hours, pick-ups, activities.
7. **Pets**: walks, training, schedules.
8. **Other fixed activities**: gym, classes, weekly meetings, events already booked (with day and time).

## Block 3: To-dos and projects
Ask them to list everything, in any order. For each one, if they know:
1. How long do you think it takes (or is it small, medium or large)?
2. Does it have a **deadline or a fixed date**? Or an **uncertain date** between two days (planned as option A/B)?
3. Does it depend on **business hours** (banks, offices, calls)?
4. What are your **2–3 priorities** for the period?

If they do not give durations, estimate them yourself and state them when delivering.

## Block 4: Recurring reminders
For each reminder:
1. What is it? (pay electricity, pay card X, clean the fridge, change the filter…)
2. How often? (every N days, on day X of each month, every week on day Y, once a year)
3. At a specific time or all day?
4. Do you want a heads-up beforehand (for example, the day before a payment)?

## Block 5: Preferences
1. At what time of day do you perform best (morning, afternoon, evening)?
2. How long can you focus on one topic in a row?
3. How much free or family time do you want? Any lighter day?
4. Do you want flexible slots for rescheduling? (default: yes, one a day)
5. Alarms on everything or only on what matters? (default: only on what matters)

---

## Example first message

> To build you the best schedule I need a few details. Let's go step by step; answer by
> number, and whatever you don't know, I'll estimate.
>
> **Block 1 – Period**
> 1. Daily, weekly, 2–3 weeks, 1–2 months, quarterly, or just reminders?
> 2. From which date and until when?
> 3. City or time zone?
> 4. Do you use Google, Apple or Outlook?
> 5. How often do you want to review the plan?
